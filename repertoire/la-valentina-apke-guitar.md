# La Valentina in Stefan Apke's 2019 guitar edition

Stefan Apke's guitar edition of Manuel María Ponce's arrangement appears
on printed p. 5 of *Cinco canciones mexicanas para guitarra*, IMSLP
561574. PDF p. 3 contains the arranger's own
`Ponce_M_M-La_Valentina.mid` attachment. The engraving credits Ponce
for the arrangement and Apke for preparing the guitar edition. IMSLP
lists Apke's edition under CC BY-SA 4.0. The Compás score data and MIDI
adaptation carry the same licence.

The print has 32 written bars in 6/8, a repeat at bar 16 and a separate
ending chord at bar 32. Apke's MIDI runs through written bars 1–16 twice,
then 17–32 once. The 48-bar export preserves all 587 pitch attacks and
their onset positions. It maps the 480 PPQ source to 24 PPQ and adds
one source tick to each gate to recover the nominal written length.
The first attack has pitches E2, B3, E4 and B4, with E2 sustained
under the moving upper notes. The last half-bar has E2, E4, G♯4 and
E5. Four independently timed MIDI tracks carry those durations. They
are playback lanes for one guitar.

Apke's MIDI is a straight quarter-note 120, equivalent to 80
dotted-quarter pulses per minute. Compás uses that tempo for the two
large beats per bar. It uses a uniform velocity and a neutral board
synth. Guitar fingerings, fermatas, dynamic curves and glissando
execution remain on the printed page. The original attachment preserves
its own guitar patch and two accented attacks.

Open **La Valentina** in the rehearsal hub for the source key and a
separate slot two semitones lower. Turn PRESETS while playing to change
key at the next written bar. The four white section keys cue played
bars 1, 9, 33 and 48. The cues are rehearsal markers. The score plays
its own accompaniment without automatic percussion or a separate canto
guide. The Rafael Gama polka, Houston ranchera and Augustine Ramirez
orquesta recordings remain separate references.
